## Reading-system debt (LP20 / LP21)

Every one of the ✅ original-language texts above was structured from raw bytes
(107 sources, 199k units — `digested/STRUCTURE-FROM-BYTES-FINDING.md`). The
**proposition** layer lags: Latin and Greek read at ~70% coverage; IAST Sanskrit
reads at 1.7–6% behind a false `clean` gate (LP20); Japanese and Arabic are
`gapped_script`. The gap inventory is the reading system's next-work list.

## Summary

- **Explicitly requested and now present:** Upanishads ✅, Bhagavad Gita ✅,
  Greek poetry (Sappho + Pindar) ✅.
- **Western canon:** Greek core ✅ complete; Latin core ✅ complete; science
  (Newton/Copernicus/Kepler-adjacent) ✅; modern-language originals partially
  present, remainder is next-pass Gutenberg/Perseus work (list above).
- **Eastern canon:** India ✅ complete on GRETIL; Japan ✅ except Heike ⊘;
  **China ⛔ all absent (ctext gated)**; MAMEC ✅ Arabic core, Persian not yet run.
- **Next pass, in order:** Chinese via a licensed CTP path → Persian via a
  working Ganjoor mirror or scraping → Plutarch *Lives* + Euclid + Chaucer +
  Dante Italian → the modern German/French originals (Kant, Hegel, Marx,
  Freud, Rousseau, Pascal).
